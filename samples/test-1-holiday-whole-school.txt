ST. XAVIER'S CONVENT SCHOOL, LUCKNOW
Circular No. SXC/2026/91                                        Date: 16 November 2026

WINTER VACATION — ALL CLASSES (NURSERY TO XII)

Dear Parents,

The school will remain closed for Winter Vacation from Thursday, 24 December 2026 to Friday, 1 January 2027 (both days inclusive) for all students from Nursery to Class XII.

The school will reopen on Monday, 4 January 2027. Regular classes and school bus services will start from the same day. The school timing after the vacation will be 8:30 AM to 2:00 PM due to the winter season.

Students are requested to complete the holiday homework uploaded on the school app and submit it to their class teacher on Monday, 4 January 2027.

The school office will remain open from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Monday, 21 December 2026 for fee payment and certificate requests.

Wishing all our students and families a safe and happy holiday season.

Sr. Maria Fernandes
Principal
