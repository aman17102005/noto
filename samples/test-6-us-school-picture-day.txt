SCHOOL PICTURE DAY IS COMING!

Dear Families,

School Picture Day for Kindergarten, Grade 1 and Grade 2 will be on Wednesday, December 9, 2026. Photos will be taken in the school library during the morning, starting at 8:45 AM.

To order photos, please complete the order form online at the school office or return the paper order form in the envelope sent home with your child. The basic package costs $25 and the deluxe package costs $40. Orders must be received by Friday, December 4, 2026. Every student will be photographed for the yearbook even if you do not order.

Please do not let your child wear green clothing, as it may not show well with the background. Students who are absent can have their photo taken on Retake Day in January (date to be announced).

If you need help with the order form in Spanish or Vietnamese, please contact our Family Liaison, Ms. Rosa Martinez, at (555) 018-4471.

Thank you,
Mr. David Nguyen
Principal
