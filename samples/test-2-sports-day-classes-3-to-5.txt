ANNUAL SPORTS DAY — CLASSES 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B AND 5A

Dear Parents,

We are happy to invite you to the Annual Sports Day of Classes 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B and 5A on Saturday, 12 December 2026 at the school ground. The programme will start at 8:30 AM and end by 12:00 noon.

Students must report to their class teacher by 7:45 AM wearing their House T-shirt, white shorts or track pants, and white sports shoes. Please send a water bottle and a light snack. A cap is strongly recommended.

A contribution of ₹100 per student for refreshments and medals must be sent in an envelope marked with the child's name and class by Friday, 4 December 2026.

Parents are welcome to watch the events. Please carry your Parent ID card for entry through Gate No. 3. Parking is not available inside the campus.

If your child has any medical condition that prevents them from taking part, please send a note signed by a parent to the class teacher by Friday, 4 December 2026.
